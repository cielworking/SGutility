COE finance guide

Understanding MAS Car Loan Limits & COE PQP Renewal

Under MAS car financing rules, buyers can finance up to 70% if OMV is $20,000 or below (30% downpayment), or up to 60% if OMV exceeds $20,000 (40% downpayment), over a maximum 7-year loan tenure.

When renewing an existing car at year 10, a 5-year PQP renewal costs 50% of the Prevailing Quota Premium but requires deregistration at year 15, while a 10-year renewal keeps future renewal flexibility.

COE & car loan questions

How is Singapore car loan monthly installment calculated?
Singapore car loans use simple flat-rate interest: Total Interest = Loan Amount × Flat Rate × Tenure (years), divided across total monthly installments.
What is the difference between 5-year and 10-year COE PQP renewal?
10-year PQP costs 100% and can be renewed again later; 5-year PQP costs 50% and cannot be renewed once the 5 years end.
What is the maximum car loan LTV limit in Singapore?
70% for cars with OMV ≤ $20,000, and 60% for cars with OMV > $20,000, capped at 7 years.
