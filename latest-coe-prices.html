COE result guide

How to read the latest Singapore COE prices

The latest bidding table shows the quota premium for Categories A, B, C, D and E and the movement from the previous exercise. This helps you see whether each vehicle category increased or decreased at the latest close.

COE is only one part of a vehicle's total cost. Dealers may apply their own package terms, and the final registration cost can include taxes, fees and the vehicle price. Confirm the official result with LTA before committing to a purchase.

COE price questions

What are the COE categories?
Categories A and B cover cars, C covers goods vehicles and buses, D covers motorcycles, and E is the open category.
What is the quota premium?
It is the price determined for a COE category at the close of a bidding exercise.
Where do the results come from?
The displayed figures use LTA data published through data.gov.sg.

Understanding the COE categories

Category A covers smaller and lower-powered cars, Category B covers larger or higher-powered cars, Category C covers goods vehicles and buses, Category D covers motorcycles, and Category E is the open category.

Always confirm final results with LTA before making a purchase decision.
