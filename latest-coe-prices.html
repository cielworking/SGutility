Singapore COE Price FAQ

How to understand the latest bidding result.

Which categories are shown?

The table covers Categories A, B, C, D and E, including the Open category.

What is the latest COE price?

It is the quota premium from the latest available bidding exercise, with previous-exercise movement when available.

Where should I verify it?

Use the official LTA or data.gov.sg source before making a vehicle purchase decision.
