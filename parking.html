Tips for finding cheaper or free parking in Singapore

  • Filter for Free Sunday & Public Holiday parking: Over 1,600 HDB carparks waive charges on Sundays and Public Holidays from 7:00 am to 10:30 pm (check the orange signboards at the gantry before leaving your vehicle).
  • Check gantry height for tall vans & roof racks: Many older HDB MSCPs have 2.0m–2.15m clearance; use the card details above to inspect height limits before entering.
  • Planning a ride or drive across the island? Pair live lots with the SPF Speed Camera Map, today’s petrol prices, and live Singapore rain radar.

Singapore parking questions

How much is motorcycle parking at HDB carparks in Singapore?
HDB motorcycle parking is generally $0.65 per daytime or overnight session, and many HDB carparks offer free Sunday and Public Holiday parking from 7:00 am to 10:30 pm.
Which carparks have live availability numbers?
SG Alerts integrates the official LTA and HDB Carpark Availability API covering roughly 2,000 live car feeds and over 200 live motorcycle lot feeds across Singapore.
What are standard HDB car parking rates?
Standard HDB carparks outside the Central Area cost $0.60 per half-hour, while Central Area carparks cost $1.20 per half-hour on Mondays to Saturdays (7:00 am to 5:00 pm). Most carparks cap overnight parking (10:30 pm to 7:00 am) at $5.00.
