COE Results Explained: November 2026 2nd Bidding (16–18 Nov)

How to read LTA COE results for the 16–18 November 2026 exercise: categories, Quota Premium vs PQP, release timing after 4pm close, and next bidding dates.

Updated 6 November 2026

Key Points (Reading COE Results)
  • Live Auto-Updated Results: View the latest LTA Quota Premiums (Cat A–E) directly on our Live COE Prices Dashboard →.
  • QP vs PQP: QP is the bidding clearing price for new registrations; PQP (3-month moving average) is for renewing an existing COE.
  • Results Release: Exercises close at 4:00 pm on the final day (usually 1st & 3rd Wednesday).

Quick Glossary

  • Quota: Number of COEs available in that category (unused quota carries forward).
  • Uniform Price: Every winner pays the same clearing QP, regardless of their higher reserve bid.
