How to book KTM Shuttle Tebrau tickets & save money

  • Book on the official KTMB KITS portal / app: Tickets typically release up to 6 months ahead. Peak Friday evening and Saturday morning trains to JB sell out weeks in advance.
  • Always book two separate one-way legs if flexible: Departing from JB Sentral charges RM5 instead of SGD 5 for the return trip (more than 3× cheaper!).
  • Passport requirement: Your passport acts as your e-ticket at the gantry — make sure it has at least 6 months validity and bring a physical copy of your passport details if renewing before travel.
  • If sold out, check Causeway buses & live cameras first: Take SMRT 950 (Woodlands Temp Interchange), SBS 170 / 170X or CW1 (Kranji MRT), or CW2 (Queen Street) after checking live Causeway traffic on our Woodlands Checkpoint Cameras or Tuas Checkpoint Cameras.

KTM Shuttle Tebrau frequently asked questions
How much does a KTM Shuttle Tebrau ticket cost from Woodlands vs JB Sentral?
One-way tickets cost S$5 when departing from Woodlands CIQ towards JB Sentral, and RM5 when departing from JB Sentral towards Singapore.
How early do boarding gates close for KTM Shuttle Tebrau?
At Woodlands CIQ, the check-in gate opens 30 minutes and closes 20 minutes before departure. At JB Sentral, gates typically open 30 minutes and close 10 minutes before departure.
What buses can I take if KTM Shuttle Tebrau tickets are sold out?
Take Causeway Link CW1 (Kranji MRT) or CW2 (Queen Street), SBS Transit 170 / 170X (Kranji MRT / Bugis), SMRT 950 (Woodlands MRT), or CW3/CW4/CW6/CW7 via Tuas Second Link. Check live checkpoint camera photos on SG Alerts before choosing Woodlands or Tuas.
