SG Arrival Card (SGAC) Entry Guide & Customs Rules

Official Immigration & Checkpoints Authority (ICA) submission rules, passport automated clearance lanes, duty-free alcohol allowances, and prohibited items checklist.

🛑 Beware of Paid Scam Sites: The SG Arrival Card is 100% FREE on the official Singapore ICA portal (eservices.ica.gov.sg/sgarrivalcard) or MyICA mobile app. Do not pay commercial intermediaries.

SG Arrival Card 3-Minute Quick Guide

📅 When to Submit

Within 3 days prior to arrival (including day of arrival). Submitting earlier than 3 days is rejected by the system. Takes under 3 minutes per traveler.

🛂 Who Must Submit

All foreign visitors entering Singapore by air, sea, or land (Causeway/Tuas). Singapore Citizens, PRs, and Long-Term Pass holders entering via land are exempt.

🤖 Automated Clearance (Automated Gates)

All foreign visitors holding biometric machine-readable passports can use Changi's automated passport clearance lanes without prior registration!

Strict Singapore Customs Rules & Allowances

Singapore strictly enforces border regulations. Knowing these rules ensures you pass through Green Channel smoothly without fines:

Category Rule / Allowance Important Note
Vapes & E-Cigarettes STRICTLY BANNED Possession fine up to S$2,000. Importing carries up to S$10,000 fine and prison. Do not pack in luggage.
Chewing Gum PROHIBITED Import of chewing gum is prohibited under Singapore law (oral dental/nicotine gum requires prescription).
Duty-Free Alcohol 1 Litre Spirits + 1L Wine OR 1L Wine + 1L Beer Must have spent at least 48 hours outside Singapore. Arriving from Malaysia has zero duty-free alcohol allowance.
Cigarettes / Tobacco Zero Allowance All tobacco brought in must be declared at the Red Channel and is subject to Singapore customs duty + GST.
Cash Declaration Max S$20,000 (or foreign equivalent) Physical cash exceeding S$20,000 must be declared on an NP727 form upon arrival.

Visitor Checklist

Changi Airport Arrival & Transport into City

Once you clear passport control and collect baggage, getting into downtown Singapore is simple and fast:

  • MRT Subway: Follow signs to Train to City in Terminal 2 or 3. Tap your phone (Apple Pay/Google Pay) or contactless Visa/Mastercard. Journey to City Hall takes ~35 minutes (~S$2.20).
  • Taxis & Ride-Hailing: Official taxi stands operate 24/7 outside every terminal. Fares to downtown average S$25–S$40 (including airport surcharge). Grab and Gojek apps are widely available.
  • Free Airport Wi-Fi: Connect to #WiFi@Changi across all terminals without requiring a local SIM card.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the SG Arrival Card free to submit?
Yes, completely free on the official ICA website (eservices.ica.gov.sg). Beware of fee-charging scam sites.
When must I submit the SG Arrival Card?
Within 3 days before arriving in Singapore (e.g. Wednesday for a Friday arrival).
Are electronic cigarettes (vapes) allowed in Singapore?
No. E-cigarettes, vaporisers, and pods are illegal and confiscated at customs with severe penalties.
