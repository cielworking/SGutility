Driving From Singapore to JB Checklist: VEP, Touch 'n Go, MyICA QR & 3/4-Tank Rule

Before driving across Woodlands Causeway or Tuas Second Link, check live Woodlands Checkpoint cameras and live Tuas Checkpoint cameras, then run through this pre-departure checklist so border clearance stays smooth.

1. Vehicle & Border Permits (VEP RFID + Touch 'n Go + Autopass)

Before approaching Woodlands or Tuas, verify all three payment and vehicle-entry items so you do not get held up at the toll booth or Malaysian CIQ lane:

  • Malaysia VEP RFID Tag: Confirm your Vehicle Entry Permit (VEP) registration is active on the JPJ portal, linked to your vehicle registration number, and paired with your Touch 'n Go eWallet with at least RM50 loaded so the RM20 Road Charge and RFID highway tolls deduct automatically.
  • Physical Touch 'n Go NFC Card: Keep a topped-up Enhanced Touch 'n Go NFC card in your car for JB shopping mall carparks (Mid Valley Southkey, Paradigm Mall, Toppen/IKEA Tebrau, KSL) and older toll plazas that do not support RFID.
  • Singapore NETS CashCard / CEPAS / ERP 2.0 OBU Card: Check your stored value before leaving so Singapore checkpoint tolls ($0.80 at Woodlands Causeway; $2.10–$3.20 at Tuas Second Link depending on time) deduct cleanly upon your return.
  • Motor Insurance & Road Tax Validity: Verify your Singapore motor insurance covers Peninsular Malaysia (most comprehensive policies include West Malaysia by default, but third-party/limited plans may require an endorsement).

2. Singapore 3/4-Tank Petrol Rule & Refuelling Strategy

Singapore-registered petrol, diesel, CNG, and hybrid vehicles departing via Woodlands or Tuas must have at least three-quarters of a tank filled before reaching Singapore immigration. Singapore Customs officers conduct random checks at departure lanes; offenders face a composition sum of up to $500 (or court prosecution) and are turned back to refuel.

Before driving north, compare today’s Singapore petrol prices and run your bank card through our Petrol Card Discount Calculator to top up at the lowest nett $/L. Full details — including EV exemptions and RON 97 rules in Malaysia — are in our dedicated Singapore 3/4-Tank Rule & JB Petrol Guide.

3. Passports, MyICA QR Code & Malaysia Immigration

  • 6-Month Passport Validity: Ensure every passenger's passport has at least six months of validity remaining and clean, undamaged pages.
  • MyICA Mobile App QR Code: Generate an individual or group QR code (up to 10 travellers in one car) before reaching the checkpoint so you can clear Singapore immigration without scanning physical passports. See our step-by-step MyICA QR clearance guide.
  • Malaysia Digital Arrival Card (MDAC) / Automated Gates: Singapore passport holders are generally exempted from MDAC for short visits and can use eligible e-gates at Bangunan Sultan Iskandar (BSI) and Sultan Abu Bakar Complex (KSAB); foreign passport holders accompanying you should verify MDAC requirements 3 days before travel.

SG to JB driving checklist FAQs

Do Singapore cars need a VEP RFID tag to drive into Johor Bahru?
Yes. Foreign-registered private cars entering Malaysia via Woodlands Causeway or Tuas Second Link require an active Malaysia Vehicle Entry Permit (VEP) RFID tag linked to a Touch 'n Go eWallet for automatic RM20 Road Charge deduction.
What is the penalty for breaching Singapore's three-quarter tank rule?
Singapore-registered petrol, diesel, and hybrid vehicles leaving via land checkpoints with less than three-quarters of a tank face a composition sum of up to $500 or prosecution in court, and drivers are turned back to refuel.
Should I still bring a physical Touch 'n Go card if I have VEP RFID?
Yes. Keep an Enhanced Touch 'n Go NFC card topped up with at least RM50 for JB shopping mall carparks, older toll booths, and backup payment if an RFID lane is temporarily unavailable.
