Driving From Singapore to JB Checklist: VEP, Touch 'n Go, MyICA QR & 3/4-Tank Rule

Before driving across Woodlands Causeway or Tuas Second Link, check live Woodlands Checkpoint cameras and live Tuas Checkpoint cameras, then run through this pre-departure checklist so border clearance stays smooth.

1. Vehicle & Border Permits (VEP + Touch 'n Go)

  • Malaysia VEP RFID Tag: Confirm your Vehicle Entry Permit (VEP) registration is active, linked to your plate number, and paired with your Touch 'n Go eWallet so the Malaysia Road Charge and highway tolls deduct automatically.
  • Physical Touch 'n Go NFC Card: Keep a topped-up physical card in your vehicle for mall carparks and older toll lanes that do not accept RFID.
  • Singapore Autopass / CashCard: Ensure enough stored value for Singapore checkpoint tolls when returning.

2. Singapore 3/4-Tank Petrol Rule

Singapore-registered petrol and diesel vehicles departing via land checkpoints must have at least three-quarters of a tank filled before immigration. Check today's Singapore petrol prices or use our Petrol Card Discount Calculator to top up at the lowest net $/L before heading north.

3. Immigration & Peak-Hour Timing

  • Prepare MyICA QR Codes: Generate your individual or group QR code before reaching the lane—see our MyICA QR clearance guide.
  • Check Both Crossings & Weather: Friday evenings, Saturday mornings, and Sunday night returns see the heaviest queues. Compare Woodlands vs Tuas and check Singapore & Johor rain radar before setting off.
