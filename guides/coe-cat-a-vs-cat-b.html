COE Cat A vs Cat B Explained: How Bidding Works and What Results Mean

Learn how LTA defines COE Category A and B, how open bidding and reserve prices work, and how to read quota premiums on OneMotoring.

Updated 6 November 2026
WhatsApp
Key Points (COE Cat A vs Cat B)
  • Category A: Petrol/hybrid cars ≤ 1,600cc & ≤ 97kW (130bhp), OR fully electric (EV) cars ≤ 110kW (147bhp).
  • Category B: Petrol/hybrid cars > 1,600cc OR > 97kW, OR EVs > 110kW.
  • Uniform Price Rule: All successful bidders pay the same clearing Quota Premium (QP) — not their maximum reserve bid.

Cat A vs Cat B vs Cat E

CategoryEligibilityTransferable (Individual)
Cat AICE/Hybrid ≤ 1,600cc & ≤ 97kW · EV ≤ 110kWNo (6-month TCOE validity)
Cat BICE/Hybrid > 1,600cc or > 97kW · EV > 110kWNo (6-month TCOE validity)
Cat E (Open)Any vehicle except motorcycles (used by dealers)Once (individual bid only)

How Bidding Works in 3 Bullets

  • Schedule: 1st & 3rd Monday of each month (12pm Mon → 4pm Wed unless a PH falls in between).
  • Deposit: S$10,000 for Cat A, B, C & E (refunded 1 working day after results if unsuccessful, minus bank admin fee).
  • Live Tools: Check Live COE Prices & PQP or estimate monthly instalments with our COE & Car Loan Calculator. Official rules: LTA OneMotoring.
