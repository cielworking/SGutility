School-Holiday JB Trips: Petrol, VEP and Causeway Tips

4 Rules Every SG Driver Must Clear Before Crossing
  • SG ¾-Tank Rule: Tank must be ≥¾ full leaving SG (fine up to S$500 + U-turn).
  • No RON95 in Malaysia: SG cars must pump RON97 (or diesel) — buyers can be penalised under KPDN enforcement.
  • JPJ VEP RFID Tag: Mandatory & actively enforced at the border (valid 5 years; TnG RFID is not a substitute).
  • RM20 Road Charge: Paid via Touch ’n Go card or eWallet only (no cash; motorcycles exempt).
