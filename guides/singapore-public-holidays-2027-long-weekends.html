Singapore Public Holidays 2027 and Long Weekends

Official MOM dates for 2027, which holidays fall next to weekends, and a practical travel checklist using SG Alerts tools.

Updated 6 November 2026

MOM published Singapore’s 11 gazetted public holidays for 2027 on 18 June 2026. This guide lists those official dates, flags natural long weekends, and ties holiday travel planning to SG Alerts checkpoint cameras, petrol and weather — without inventing leave-policy outcomes for your workplace.

Official 2027 public holidays (MOM)

Source: MOM press release — Public Holidays for 2027 (18 June 2026). The same dates appear on MOM’s Public holidays page.

  • New Year’s Day — Friday, 1 January 2027
  • Chinese New Year — Saturday, 6 February 2027 and Sunday, 7 February 2027; MOM’s release states that Monday, 8 February 2027, will be a public holiday
  • Hari Raya Puasa — Wednesday, 10 March 2027
  • Good Friday — Friday, 26 March 2027
  • Labour Day — Saturday, 1 May 2027
  • Hari Raya Haji — Monday, 17 May 2027
  • Vesak Day — Thursday, 20 May 2027
  • National Day — Monday, 9 August 2027
  • Deepavali — Thursday, 28 October 2027
  • Christmas Day — Saturday, 25 December 2027

MOM states that employees covered by the Employment Act are entitled to these 11 paid public holidays, that employers and employees may mutually agree to substitute a holiday for another working day, and that work-on-holiday pay / time-off-in-lieu rules differ by employee group. Read the Employment Act guidance on MOM — this guide does not interpret your contract. Source: same MOM press release.

Natural long weekends (calendar reading only)

These observations follow from the gazetted weekdays above. They are not MOM “leave packages” — whether you get Friday or Monday off still depends on your roster and any substitution agreement.

  • New Year’s Day (Fri 1 Jan): Fri–Sun block for many weekday workers if Saturday/Sunday are already rest days.
  • Chinese New Year: Sat–Sun gazetted days plus Monday 8 Feb as a public holiday per MOM — a multi-day CNY window; road and checkpoint demand often rises around this stretch historically, so plan cams early rather than assuming empty roads.
  • Good Friday (Fri 26 Mar): Fri–Sun style weekend for many.
  • Hari Raya Haji (Mon 17 May) and National Day (Mon 9 Aug): Monday holidays that create Sat–Mon style long weekends when Sat/Sun are rest days.
  • Vesak (Thu 20 May) and Deepavali (Thu 28 Oct): mid-week / Thursday holidays — some people bridge with annual leave; that is a personal leave choice, not a gazetted long weekend.
  • Labour Day (Sat 1 May) and Christmas (Sat 25 Dec): Saturday holidays; weekday workers should check MOM/employer rules on rest-day overlap rather than assuming an automatic Monday off.

For school-term calendars (different from gazetted PH), use MOE’s published school holiday dates — not this list. School-holiday Causeway behaviour is covered separately in year-end Causeway timing and JB petrol, VEP and tank tips.

Holiday travel checklist with SG Alerts tools

  1. Checkpoint cams: Before a JB run, open Woodlands and Tuas live images and compare timing — see also Woodlands vs Tuas.
  2. Petrol: Check Singapore petrol prices the day before; for how to read boards, use how to check petrol prices. Cross-border fuel and VEP rules belong in the JB tips guide, not here.
  3. Weather / haze: Storms and haze still happen on holidays. Use rain radar and PSI; for band meanings see PSI and haze what to do.
  4. ERP: OneMotoring states there are no ERP charges on Saturdays, Sundays and public holidays — useful for on-island drives on a PH, but confirm eve-of-holiday shortened hours on the ERP page if you drive the day before. See how to check ERP rates.

CNY 2027 detail worth reading twice

MOM’s 2027 release lists Chinese New Year on Saturday 6 February and Sunday 7 February, with a footnote that Monday 8 February 2027 will be a public holiday. MOM’s general public-holidays page also discusses rest-day wording for Sunday holidays elsewhere in the calendar. For planning, treat Mon 8 Feb 2027 as part of the CNY public-holiday window per the press release, and confirm any workplace substitution with HR. Sources: MOM PH 2027 release; MOM public holidays.

Checkpoint and petrol habits that beat “leave earlier lah”

Long weekends do not have a single safe departure hour. Patterns change with school holidays, weather and one-off events. A more reliable habit:

  • Night before: skim Woodlands and Tuas timestamps so you know what “normal” looked like.
  • Departure morning: re-open both camera pages; if one side is clearly worse, reread Woodlands vs Tuas before you commit.
  • Fuel: set a board-price check on petrol prices the evening before; confirm pole price at the station. Cross-border tank and VEP rules stay in the JB tips guide.
  • Documents: MyICA QR and clearance steps are in MyICA QR clearance — pack that checklist before the holiday rush, not at the booth.

Year-end school holidays overlap some December/January travel even when Christmas is a Saturday holiday — use the year-end Causeway guide for that peak, and this page for gazetted PH dating.

Weather on a public holiday

Public holidays do not pause convective storms or haze. If outdoor plans matter, open rain radar and the Singapore / Malaysia rain-forecast pages, and check regional PSI. For interpreting unhealthy bands, use PSI and haze: what to do. Do not treat a clear morning sky as a forecast.

What this page will not do

  • Invent “best leave dates” or guarantee empty Causeway slots.
  • Replace MOM’s Employment Act FAQs on holiday pay.
  • Replace MOE school holiday PDFs.
  • Replace ICA / Customs / JPJ rules for leaving Singapore by land.

Bottom line: Anchor 2027 leave planning on MOM’s gazetted list, then use SG Alerts cams, petrol and weather when the holiday involves a road trip. Re-check MOM if a special holiday (for example a past polling day) is gazetted later — 2027’s published set above is the baseline from the June 2026 release.
