Northeast Monsoon Singapore: Flash Floods and PUB Alerts

What the Northeast Monsoon is, the latest MSS outlook, monsoon surges, and how to use official PUB flood alerts — without inventing hotspot lists or rainfall totals.

Updated 6 November 2026
WhatsApp
Key Points (Northeast Monsoon)
  • Season Window: December – early March — early/wet phase (Dec–early Jan) brings prolonged monsoon surges; late/dry phase (late Jan–early Mar) is windier and drier (MSS).
  • Monsoon Surges: Typically 2–4 events per season, lasting 1–5 days of widespread rain.
  • Flash Floods: Localised heavy-rain floods usually subside within an hour; never drive through water above kerb height or hiding road markings (PUB).

Quick Motorist Safety Rules (PUB)

  • Turn back if floodwater is above road-kerb height or road markings are not visible.
  • If passable & unavoidable: Drive slowly and steadily in the middle lane, one vehicle at a time; gently tap brakes after exiting to dry them.
  • If stalled in rising water: Do not restart the engine — switch on hazards, move to higher ground, and call for help.
