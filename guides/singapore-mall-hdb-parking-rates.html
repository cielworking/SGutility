Practical Parking Tips

  • Free Sunday & Public Holiday parking: Over 1,600 HDB carparks waive charges on Sundays and Public Holidays from 7:00 am to 10:30 pm (always verify the orange gantry signboard on arrival).
  • Check MSCP gantry height: Older HDB multi-storey carparks can have 2.0m–2.15m clearance; check the gantry badge on each card in our map finder before entering with roof racks or vans.
