Year-End School Holidays and Causeway Timing

MOE year-end school holidays 21 Nov–31 Dec 2026, Christmas and New Year public holidays, and how to check Woodlands and Tuas cameras before you cross — without guessing unannounced ICA peaks.

Updated 23 November 2026
WhatsApp
Key Points (2026–27 Year-End)
  • ICA Peak Advisory: 20 Nov 2026 – 3 Jan 2027 — expect heavy traffic at Woodlands & Tuas, with car queues up to 3 hours during peaks.
  • 2 Long Weekends: Christmas (Fri 25 Dec 2026) & New Year’s Day (Fri 1 Jan 2027) — both fall on Fridays.
  • Document Check: Verify every passenger’s physical passport (6+ months validity) matches their MyICA QR code before leaving — mismatches are barred from departing until fixed.

Before You Leave (Quick Checklist)

  • Check live cams & AI queue counts first: SG–JB Checkpoints Hub · Woodlands Cams · Tuas Cams.
  • Generate MyICA QR at home (individual or group) and still bring physical passports — MyICA QR guide.
  • Sort fuel & VEP before queuing: ≥¾ tank in SG, activated JPJ VEP RFID tag, and topped-up Touch ’n Go (RM20 Road Charge) — JB petrol & VEP tips.
  • Check live rain radar: Wet approach roads slow merging even when booths look clear.
