Year-End School Holidays and Causeway Timing

MOE year-end school holidays 21 Nov–31 Dec 2026, Christmas and New Year public holidays, and how to check Woodlands and Tuas cameras before you cross — without guessing unannounced ICA peaks.

Updated 6 November 2026
WhatsApp

The stretch from late November into early January is when many Singapore households plan Johor trips, longer Malaysia drives, or visits home across the Causeway and Second Link. Congestion can be heavy — but the honest way to plan is to lock the calendar facts from MOE and MOM, watch for any ICA advisory when one is published, and check live cameras on the day you travel. This guide does not invent peak hours or wait times.

For fuel rules, Malaysia VEP / RFID, Touch ’n Go Road Charge, and RON95 restrictions, use the companion guide School-holiday JB trips: petrol, VEP and Causeway tips — keep this page for timing and camera habits.

The dates that are confirmed

MOE year-end school holidays

According to the Ministry of Education academic calendar:

  • MOE Kindergarten, Primary and Secondary — Term 4 school holidays from Saturday, 21 November 2026 to Thursday, 31 December 2026.
  • Post-secondary (Years 1–3 / JC & MI bands listed on the calendar) — Term 4 school holidays from Saturday, 28 November 2026 to Thursday, 31 December 2026.

Source: MOE academic calendar.

MOE’s press release on 2027 school terms states that the 2027 school year for MOE primary schools (including MOE Kindergartens) and secondary schools starts on Monday, 4 January 2027. The same release notes Primary 1 and Kindergarten 1 start on Monday, 4 January 2027, while Primary 2 to 6 and Kindergarten 2 start on Tuesday, 5 January 2027. Source: School Terms and Holidays for 2027 (MOE). That means the bridge from New Year’s weekend into the new term is short — useful if you are returning for school reopen.

Public holidays inside / beside that window

From the Ministry of Manpower public holidays list:

  • Christmas Day — Friday, 25 December 2026
  • New Year’s Day — Friday, 1 January 2027

Sources: MOM — Public holidays; Public Holidays for 2027 (MOM).

Because both Christmas 2026 and New Year’s Day 2027 fall on Fridays, each creates a Friday–Sunday block for many weekday workers without needing annual leave. That is a calendar observation only — it is not a forecast of checkpoint volume.

Past context: Deepavali long weekend (6–9 November 2026)

ICA did publish a land-checkpoint advisory for the Deepavali long weekend. That advisory covered Friday, 6 November to Monday, 9 November 2026, expected heavy traffic at Woodlands and Tuas, and encouraged travellers to use non-peak hours such as early mornings and late evenings, to check traffic before leaving, and to use MyICA QR codes. Source: ICA — Heavy Traffic Expected at Land Checkpoints during Upcoming Deepavali Long Weekend.

That Deepavali window is now past. Use it only as a reminder that ICA sometimes issues holiday-specific advisories with practical tips — not as a copy-paste prediction for Christmas, New Year, or the rest of the school holidays.

Has ICA published a year-end 2026 / Christmas–NY advisory yet?

TBC — none verified for the 21 Nov 2026–31 Dec 2026 school holidays (or Christmas / New Year 2026–27) as of 6 November 2026. We re-checked ICA’s media room and the older “year-end school holidays” advisory URL; that older page still describes the prior season’s dates (21 November 2025 to 1 January 2026), so it is not a 2026 year-end notice.

Until ICA publishes a fresh advisory covering this year’s break, do not treat third-party “typical peak Friday” charts as official forecasts. Watch ICA’s news and media room closer to your travel date, and rely on live cameras the day you go.

What to do instead of guessing peak hours

  1. Mark the school-holiday blocks (21 Nov–31 Dec for MK/Pri/Sec; 28 Nov–31 Dec for listed post-secondary bands) and the two Friday public holidays (25 Dec 2026, 1 Jan 2027).
  2. Decide Woodlands vs Tuas by geography and live conditions, not habit. See Woodlands vs Tuas.
  3. Check both camera pages before you leave: Woodlands traffic cameras and Tuas traffic cameras. Look at the timestamps; an old frame is not useful.
  4. Prepare MyICA QR codes early — MyICA QR clearance guide and ICA’s QR page (last updated 27 Oct 2026 on that page).
  5. Bring passports even if you clear Singapore immigration by QR.
  6. Complete petrol / VEP / Touch ’n Go checks via the JB petrol & VEP tips guide before you join the queue.
  7. Watch for construction and diversions when ICA or on-site marshals advise them. Conditions change; follow signs on the day.

How to read the cameras without over-claiming

Camera images show Singapore-side approach and plaza conditions in the frame. They do not measure Malaysian immigration time, and they do not publish an official “minutes to clear” number. Use them as a go / wait / switch-checkpoint signal:

  • If Woodlands looks heavily queued and Tuas looks clearer (or the reverse), factor in your extra driving time across Singapore before you switch.
  • If both look heavy, delaying the trip or shifting to a less popular hour is often wiser than refreshing social media rumours.
  • Pair cameras with weather on the SG Alerts dashboard and rain radar — wet roads can slow merging even when booths look free.

A calm year-end crossing checklist

  • School holidays (MK/Pri/Sec): 21 Nov–31 Dec 2026 (MOE)
  • Post-secondary Term 4 holidays: 28 Nov–31 Dec 2026 (MOE calendar)
  • Christmas PH: Fri 25 Dec 2026 (MOM)
  • New Year PH: Fri 1 Jan 2027 (MOM); 2027 school year starts 4 Jan 2027 (MOE), with staggered starts noted on the MOE release
  • ICA year-end / Christmas–NY advisory: TBC — watch ICA media room
  • MyICA QR generated; passports packed
  • Woodlands and Tuas cameras checked with fresh timestamps
  • Petrol / VEP / Road Charge: see JB tips companion guide

Bottom line: The calendar is fixed by MOE and MOM. Checkpoint load is not something this page will invent. When ICA publishes a year-end advisory, follow it; until then, prepare documents and QR codes, then decide with Woodlands and Tuas live cameras on the day.
