When Is the Next COE Bidding? November 2026 to January 2027 Dates

LTA COE open bidding dates from November 2026 to January 2027, how the Monday–Wednesday window works, and where to check results after each exercise closes.

Updated 6 November 2026
WhatsApp

Singapore’s Certificate of Entitlement (COE) open bidding usually runs twice a month. If you are buying a car, renewing plans, or just watching Category A and B premiums, the useful question is not only “what was the last Quota Premium?” but “when does the next exercise open and close?”

This page lists the official Land Transport Authority (LTA) / OneMotoring dates for November 2026 through January 2027, explains the normal Monday–Wednesday pattern, and points you to where results appear. Category definitions and bidding mechanics are covered in our sister guide, COE Cat A vs Cat B explained.

How the bidding window usually works

LTA’s OneMotoring COE page states that there are two open bidding exercises a month. They usually start at 12 noon on the first and third Monday of the month and last three working days, ending at 4pm on Wednesday of the same week if there is no public holiday in between. Exact start and end times for each exercise are published in LTA’s yearly schedule PDFs.

Sources: OneMotoring — Certificate of Entitlement (COE); OneMotoring — COE Open Bidding.

LTA also publishes the quota available for each category before an exercise. For the November 2026 to January 2027 bidding period, LTA’s news release sets the overall quota and notes that bidding under that quota started on 2 November 2026, with exercises on the first and third Monday of each month, and a longer three-week gap between the second November exercise and the first December exercise. Source: LTA COE quota for November 2026 to January 2027.

Official dates: November 2026 – January 2027

The following start and end times are taken from LTA’s published schedules (Singapore time). Each exercise opens at 12 noon on the start date and closes at 4pm on the end date.

Source PDF: Vehicle Quota System Schedule of COE Open Bidding Exercises From January to December 2026 (OneMotoring / LTA).

  • November 2026 (1st): Mon 2 Nov 2026, 12 noon → Wed 4 Nov 2026, 4pm — this exercise has already ended. OneMotoring’s open-bidding page showed it ended on 04/11/2026 16:00:00 hrs and published that round’s results there.
  • November 2026 (2nd): Mon 16 Nov 2026, 12 noon → Wed 18 Nov 2026, 4pm
  • December 2026 (1st): Mon 7 Dec 2026, 12 noon → Wed 9 Dec 2026, 4pm — the 2026 schedule marks this exercise with a note that there is a longer interval of three weeks after the previous exercise.

If LTA revises a date because of a public holiday or operational change, the PDF and the live OneMotoring open-bidding page take priority over this summary. Bookmark those official files before you plan a dealer appointment around a close.

When do results come out?

LTA’s COE page says you will know if your bid is successful when the bidding results are announced, and OneMotoring’s COE Open Bidding page is where the latest exercise’s Quota Premiums (QP), quota, and bid counts are displayed after that exercise ends.

What we can say from official materials: each listed exercise closes at 4pm on its end date. After close, results are published on the OneMotoring open-bidding page (the November 2026 first exercise, for example, is shown there as having ended at 16:00:00 hrs, with a full results table).

What we cannot pin to an official minute: in the LTA/OneMotoring pages reviewed for this guide, LTA does not publish a fixed “results always appear at 4:0X pm” clock. Secondary websites often say results appear shortly after close, but that timing is not treated here as an LTA guarantee. Practical approach: from 4pm on the closing Wednesday, refresh OneMotoring’s open-bidding page, then check SG Alerts latest COE prices, which mirrors the published premiums once they are available.

If you bid yourself, OneMotoring also provides an Enquire/Revise COE Bid digital service (Singpass login) to check bid status during the exercise. Dealer packages may notify you separately — that is a private arrangement, not an LTA timetable.

How to use the calendar as a buyer

  1. Mark the next open and close from the list above. The soonest remaining 2026 exercise after the first November round is 16–18 November 2026.
  2. Decide whether you need this exercise or the next. A longer gap after the second November round means December’s first exercise starts on 7 December — plan financing and dealer paperwork with that spacing in mind.
  3. Confirm Cat A vs Cat B for the exact model (engine capacity and maximum power rules). See Cat A vs Cat B and LTA’s category notes on the open-bidding page.
  4. Watch several exercises on latest COE prices before treating one QP as “the” market level.
  5. On closing day, check OneMotoring after 4pm rather than relying on chat-group screenshots.

Where SG Alerts fits

SG Alerts is not the bidding system. It is a compact results view so you can compare Categories A–E and the change from the previous exercise without digging through multiple OneMotoring panels. Always treat LTA/OneMotoring as authoritative if figures ever disagree, and use the schedule PDFs linked above for dates.

Bottom line: Next open exercises after early November 2026 are 16–18 Nov, 7–9 Dec, and 21–23 Dec 2026, then 4–6 Jan and 18–20 Jan 2027, each noon to 4pm as published by LTA. Results appear on OneMotoring after each close — refresh from 4pm onward, then open the COE prices dashboard.
