February 2027 BTO and the HFE Letter Deadline (15 December 2026)

What HDB has announced for the February 2027 BTO exercise, why you need an HFE letter, the 15 December 2026 document deadline, and a step-by-step checklist from HDB pages.

Updated 6 November 2026
WhatsApp

If you want to ballot in HDB’s February 2027 Build-To-Order (BTO) exercise, the paperwork deadline that matters first is not the launch week itself — it is getting a valid HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter, with documents in, by the date HDB has already flagged: 15 December 2026.

This guide sticks to what HDB and reputable reportage of HDB’s launch materials have published. Where an exact February start date or full price list is not out yet, we say so.

What HDB has announced for February 2027

On the HDB Flat Portal’s upcoming BTO notice (as reported when the November 2026 exercise launched), HDB said the next BTO sales exercise offering about 4,300 flats will be held in February 2027, with a concurrent Sale of Balance Flats (SBF) exercise offering about 5,000 flats. HDB also told prospective buyers to apply for an HFE letter early and submit all required documents by 15 December 2026. Source portal: HDB Flat Portal — BTO. CNA’s 4 November 2026 report of the same launch materials repeats those figures and the HFE document deadline: CNA — November 2026 BTO launch coverage.

Towns named for the February 2027 BTO exercise in that coverage include Bishan, Tampines, Tengah and Toa Payoh. The Straits Times (5 November 2026) reported HDB will launch about 4,300 BTO flats across five projects in those four towns, including a second Mount Pleasant project in Toa Payoh. Source: The Straits Times — Feb 2027 BTO.

Not announced yet (as of this guide): the exact February 2027 application open/close clock times, full flat-type mix and selling prices for every project, and later 2027 exercise months. HDB typically publishes those at each launch — watch the Flat Portal and SG Alerts BTO launches.

What an HFE letter is

HDB’s sales-exercise FAQs state that all potential flat buyers must obtain a valid HFE letter before they can apply for a new flat in a sales exercise. The letter is a comprehensive assessment of eligibility to buy an HDB flat, and of housing grants and/or an HDB housing loan. Second-timers are also informed about any resale levy or premium payable when buying a subsidised flat from HDB.

HDB encourages applicants to apply early because the HFE letter is valid for 9 months, and processing takes about a month after HDB receives the full set of required documents (longer before and during sales months). Sources: HDB FAQs on sales exercises; MyNiceHome HFE overview How to apply for an HFE letter.

Who needs it

  • Anyone who wants to apply for a new flat in a BTO or other HDB sales exercise — you need a valid HFE letter before you can submit the flat application.
  • Households planning an HDB housing loan and/or grants — the HFE process assesses those outcomes together.
  • If you only completed Step 1 (preliminary check), that is not enough to apply for a flat; you must complete Step 2 and receive the letter.

HDB also notes that during an 8-day sales application period the “Apply for HFE Letter” e-Service is temporarily unavailable. Buyers who already hold a valid HFE letter, or who already submitted an HFE application before the sales exercise, are not blocked from the flat application by that pause. Source: HDB Flat Portal upcoming-BTO notice / CNA report linked above.

Step-by-step checklist (HDB’s two steps)

Step 1 — Check preliminary HFE

On the HDB Flat Portal, run the preliminary eligibility check for a quick overview of flat, grant and loan outcomes. This guides planning; it is not the final letter.

Step 2 — Apply for the HFE letter (within 30 calendar days)

HDB’s HFE help pages state that Step 1 and Step 2 must be completed within 30 calendar days of each other, including e-signing by co-applicants/occupiers via Singpass and uploading required documents. If you miss that window, start again from Step 1. Source: HFE letter application process (HDB).

Documents — what HDB publishes

  • Use Myinfo where offered to reduce form-filling; HDB may still SMS you if extra supporting documents are needed (for example more than one job).
  • Upload files that are PDF, JPG or PNG, not encrypted; each file up to 5 MB; all files up to 40 MB total; filenames limited to letters, numbers, spaces, dashes, hyphens or full stops.
  • Documents in English, Chinese, Malay or Tamil are accepted; other languages need an official English translation with the original.
  • Income assessment uses HDB’s published employment/income guidelines (including the assessment period rules on HDB’s income documents page). Source: Income guidelines and documents.

After you submit

  • Keep the acknowledgement details from the portal.
  • Watch SMS/email if HDB asks for more documents; upload via My Flat Dashboard as HDB instructs.
  • When the letter is ready, view it on the Flat Portal. Aim to finish document submission by 15 December 2026 if you intend to join the February 2027 exercises.

Practical timeline for Feb 2027 hopefuls

  1. Complete Step 1 + Step 2 as early as you can in November/early December 2026.
  2. Upload every requested document; do not wait for the sales week.
  3. Target HDB’s 15 December 2026 document deadline for Feb 2027 interest.
  4. Track towns and application rates on SG Alerts BTO launches and the official portal when February opens.
  5. Remember: a computer ballot, not first-come-first-served, decides shortlists for BTO applications (HDB’s standard sales rule).

Bottom line: February 2027 is confirmed as the next BTO window with about 4,300 flats (plus about 5,000 SBF) across Bishan, Tampines, Tengah and Toa Payoh. Exact launch clock times are not published yet. Your controllable deadline is the HFE letter — finish documents by 15 December 2026.
