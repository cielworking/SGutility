February 2027 BTO and the HFE Letter Deadline (15 December 2026)

What HDB has confirmed for the February 2027 BTO exercise, which towns have been named, and why the HFE document date of 15 December 2026 comes first. Launch dates and prices remain TBC.

Updated 12 November 2026
WhatsApp

If you want to ballot in HDB’s February 2027 Build-To-Order (BTO) exercise, the paperwork deadline that matters first is not the launch week itself — it is getting a valid HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter, with documents in, by the date HDB has already flagged: 15 December 2026.

This guide sticks to what HDB has published (and, where noted, news reports of HDB’s launch materials). Where an exact February application window, prices, or project-level unit counts are not on an official HDB page we could re-verify, we mark them TBC.

What HDB has announced for February 2027

The HDB Flat Portal’s upcoming BTO notice has stated that the next Build-To-Order sales exercise offering about 4,300 flats will be held in February 2027, with a concurrent Sale of Balance Flats (SBF) exercise offering about 5,000 flats. HDB also tells prospective buyers to apply for an HFE letter early and submit all required documents by 15 December 2026 if they want to take part. During the 8-day application period, HDB says the “Apply for HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) Letter” e-Service will be temporarily unavailable; that does not affect buyers who already have a valid HFE letter or who submitted an HFE application before the sales exercise. Source: HDB Flat Portal — BTO.

Re-check note (12 November 2026): A direct fetch of the Flat Portal BTO page returned an error this morning, so treat the wording above as last confirmed from HDB’s published notice (and contemporaneous reports of that notice) rather than a fresh 12 Nov screenshot. Re-open the portal before you rely on any figure. No new official launch date, price list, or flat-classification table was found between 6 and 12 November 2026.

Towns named for the February 2027 BTO exercise in HDB launch materials, as reported when the November 2026 exercise opened, are Bishan, Tampines, Tengah and Toa Payoh — four towns, with coverage describing five projects (including a second Mount Pleasant project in Toa Payoh). Sources: HDB Flat Portal notice; CNA coverage of the November 2026 launch materials; The Straits Times, 5 November 2026 (reporting HDB’s 4 November materials).

Project-level unit mixes, waiting times, and Standard / Plus / Prime labels that appear in news articles are not copied here as official counts. Confirm any project page on the HDB Flat Portal. This guide keeps HDB’s official “about 4,300” / “about 5,000” wording only.

What is still TBC

  • Exact February 2027 application open and close dates and times
  • Selling prices and official flat classifications
  • Project-level flat counts and names until they appear on an official HDB project page you can open yourself
  • Later 2027 BTO exercise months

HDB typically publishes those at each launch. Watch the Flat Portal and SG Alerts BTO launches. After application rates are out, use How to read BTO application rates — do not treat a rate as a chance-of-success promise.

The November 2026 exercise is now past

The November 2026 BTO application window was published as closing on 11 November 2026. That deadline has passed. This page is about preparing for February 2027, starting with the HFE document date of 15 December 2026.

What an HFE letter is

HDB’s sales-exercise FAQs state that all potential flat buyers must obtain a valid HFE letter before they can apply for a new flat in a sales exercise. The letter is a comprehensive assessment of eligibility to buy an HDB flat, and of housing grants and/or an HDB housing loan. Second-timers are also informed about any resale levy or premium payable when buying a subsidised flat from HDB.

HDB encourages applicants to apply early because the HFE letter is valid for 9 months, and processing takes about a month after HDB receives the full set of required documents (longer before and during sales months). Sources: HDB FAQs on sales exercises; MyNiceHome HFE overview How to apply for an HFE letter.

Who needs it

  • Anyone who wants to apply for a new flat in a BTO or other HDB sales exercise — you need a valid HFE letter before you can submit the flat application.
  • Households planning an HDB housing loan and/or grants — the HFE process assesses those outcomes together.

Follow HDB’s own eligibility rules for your household type. This guide does not decide whether you qualify.

Step-by-step checklist (HDB’s two steps)

HDB’s HFE help pages describe a two-step flow. Step 1 and Step 2 must be completed within 30 calendar days of each other, including e-signing by co-applicants/occupiers via Singpass and uploading required documents. Source: HDB’s HFE letter application process (linked from the Flat Portal / HFE e-Service help).

Step 1 — Check preliminary HFE

Use HDB’s e-Service to run the preliminary check and see what documents you will need. Do this early enough that the 30-day clock still leaves time to gather papers before 15 December 2026.

Step 2 — Apply for the HFE letter (within 30 calendar days)

Submit the application, have co-applicants and occupiers e-sign where HDB requires, and upload the documents HDB lists for your case. Keep the 30-day rule in mind — starting Step 1 in mid-December may be too tight if anything is missing.

Documents — what HDB publishes

Required documents depend on your household and income sources. Use the document list inside the HFE e-Service / HDB help pages — this guide will not invent a checklist of bank statements or CPF pages. If HDB later asks for more documents, follow the request on My Flat Dashboard.

After you submit

  • Keep the acknowledgement details from the portal.
  • Watch SMS/email if HDB asks for more documents; upload via My Flat Dashboard as HDB instructs.
  • When the letter is ready, view it on the Flat Portal. Aim to finish document submission by 15 December 2026 if you intend to join the February 2027 exercises.

Practical timeline for Feb 2027 hopefuls

  1. Now–mid December 2026: Complete HFE Step 1 and Step 2, including documents, targeting HDB’s 15 December 2026 submission date.
  2. After 15 December: If you already submitted, wait for the letter and keep it valid (9-month validity per HDB). If you missed the date, read the latest HDB notice before assuming you can still apply in February.
  3. February 2027: When HDB publishes the exact launch window, apply only with a valid HFE letter. Open/close clock times remain TBC until then.
  4. After the exercise, application rates belong on how to read application rates and on BTO launches — not as a second “launch dates” article today.

Bottom line: February 2027 is confirmed as the next BTO window with about 4,300 flats (plus about 5,000 SBF) in Bishan, Tampines, Tengah and Toa Payoh. Exact launch clock times, prices and official project-level counts are TBC. The deadline you can act on now is the HFE letter — finish documents by 15 December 2026, and re-check the HDB Flat Portal before you treat any figure as final.
