Best Credit Cards for Petrol in Singapore: Shell, Esso, Caltex, SPC & Sinopec Compared

In Singapore, headline pump prices across Shell, Esso, Caltex, SPC, and Sinopec are often within $0.03–$0.06 per litre of each other. What actually separates a cheap pump from an expensive one is which credit card and station loyalty tier you stack — saving between 14% and 24.2% per fill-up.

How Petrol Discounts Actually Stack (Don't Multiply Wrong)

A common mistake is adding instant site discounts and bank cashback linearly. In reality, discounts stack multiplicatively:

  1. Upfront Station Discount: Deducted immediately off the gross pump bill (for example, 14%–18% off when flashing your loyalty app + bank card).
  2. Bank Statement Cashback / Rebate: Calculated on the remaining net charge billed to your credit card (for example, an extra 5%–8% cashback on the post-discount total).
  3. Loyalty Points: Smiles, Shell GO+, or Caltex Rewards points add a small effective extra 0.5%–1.5% when redeemed for fuel vouchers.

Example: An 18% upfront site discount plus 6% bank cashback yields 1 - (0.82 × 0.94) = 22.92% effective total savings — not 24%.

Matching Your Driving Profile to the Right Card

  • High-mileage drivers (200+ litres/month): DBS Esso Card or OCBC 365 (Sinopec/Caltex) typically deliver the lowest net $/L because you easily clear monthly minimum spend and unlock bonus fuel rebates.
  • All-in-one cashback households: If you already use the UOB One Card (and UOB One Account) for groceries, Shopee, Grab, and utilities, pumping at Shell keeps your spend consolidated without managing another card.
  • Motorcyclists & weekend drivers (<$150/month fuel): Skip cards with strict $800+ monthly minimums. Use Smart Energy member pricing or SPC + POSB Everyday so you never forfeit cashback on quiet months.
  • RON 92 vs RON 95 vs Shell V-Power: If your owner's manual allows RON 92 (available at Esso, SPC, and Caltex), dropping from RON 95 to RON 92 saves another 1%–2% effortlessly. Drivers wanting premium detergents (Shell V-Power, Caltex Platinum 98, Sinopec SINO X Power) should check today’s Premium column before pumping.

Before Driving to Johor Bahru

Remember Singapore’s mandatory three-quarter tank petrol rule for Singapore-registered petrol, diesel, and hybrid cars exiting via Woodlands or Tuas. Topping up with your best petrol card at a neighbourhood station before approaching the expressway avoids long queues at the final Woodlands/Tuas kiosks.

Singapore petrol credit card FAQs

Which credit card gives the highest petrol discount in Singapore?
DBS Esso Card offers up to 24.2% savings at Esso (with Smiles Savings and minimum spend), OCBC 365 offers up to 24% at Sinopec and 22.92% at Caltex, UOB One offers up to 21.15% at Shell, and POSB Everyday offers up to 20.1% at SPC. Budget member stations like Smart Energy can still undercut major brands without meeting a monthly credit card minimum spend.
Why is my actual petrol rebate lower than the advertised 21%–24%?
Headline petrol savings stack upfront station discounts (applied on the gross amount) with credit card statement cashback (applied on the net amount paid after the upfront discount) and require meeting a monthly minimum spend tier. If you miss the minimum spend, you only receive the upfront station discount.
Which petrol station is cheapest if I do not want a minimum spend credit card?
Smart Energy (with member pricing) and SPC (using an SPC&U card plus a no-minimum-spend card like POSB Everyday) are typically the most predictable choices for low-mileage drivers and motorcyclists who do not hit $600–$1,000 in monthly card spend.
