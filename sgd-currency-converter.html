Singapore Money Guide

Paying in Singapore: Cash, Cards & Money Changers

1. Contactless Cards Everywhere

Singapore is virtually cashless. Visa and Mastercard (via card, Apple Pay, Google Pay) work on buses, MRT trains (SimplyGo), taxis, and retail stores with zero ticket queues.

2. Keep S$30–S$50 Cash for Hawkers

While many hawkers accept local PayNow QR, foreign banking apps cannot scan SGQR. Having modest physical cash ensures you can eat at historic hawker centres smoothly.

3. Best Money Changer Hubs

Avoid airport cash counters if exchanging larger amounts. The Arcade (Raffles Place MRT), People's Park Complex (Chinatown), and Lucky Plaza offer top competitive spreads.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use foreign credit cards, Apple Pay or Google Pay in Singapore?
Yes! Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay and Google Pay work throughout Singapore. You do not even need to buy a transport card; just tap your card or phone directly at the MRT turnstiles.
Where can I find the best money changers in Singapore?
The Arcade at Raffles Place, People's Park Complex in Chinatown, and Lucky Plaza on Orchard Road have clusters of competitive changers with tight spreads.
Do I need to buy an EZ-Link card for public transport in Singapore?
No. Visitors can tap any contactless Mastercard or Visa card (or Apple Pay/Google Pay linked card) directly on MRT and buses via the SimplyGo system.
