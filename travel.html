Peak-Hour Crossing Guide

Best Times to Cross Woodlands & Tuas Checkpoint

Before leaving for Johor Bahru or returning to Singapore, check both the live Woodlands Causeway cameras and Tuas Second Link cameras alongside the 4-week Travel Time Patterns chart above.

  • Woodlands (Cam 2701 & 2702): Typically busiest on weekday mornings into Singapore (6:00 am – 9:00 am), Friday evenings into JB (5:30 pm – 10:00 pm), and Saturday mornings (7:30 am – 12:30 pm).
  • Tuas Second Link (Cam 4703 & 4713): Often faster for western Singapore (Jurong/Bukit Batok) and Bukit Indah/Gelang Patah, though Cam 4713 can still back up on Friday nights and public holiday eves.
  • Best low-traffic windows: Weekday mid-day (10:30 am – 3:00 pm) and late nights (after 10:30 pm) usually show Smooth vehicle counts on YOLOX AI.

Woodlands & Tuas Checkpoint questions

Which camera shows the queue towards JB at Tuas Checkpoint?
Camera 4713 shows the main outbound viaduct heading toward Johor Bahru on the right, while Camera 4703 shows incoming traffic toward Singapore on the upper-right lanes.
How accurate is the AI vehicle count?
YOLOX-S counts visible vehicles on LTA road approach cameras every 5 minutes. Indoor immigration hall counters and motorcycle lanes inside the complex may still differ.
When should I switch from Woodlands to Tuas?
If Woodlands shows Heavy (46+ vehicles) on Cam 2702 and Tuas Cam 4713 is Smooth or Moderate, switching to Tuas can save 30–60 minutes depending on your JB destination.
