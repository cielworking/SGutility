Second Link travel guide

How to read Tuas Checkpoint cameras & Second Link traffic

Review the live Tuas Checkpoint traffic camera images before crossing via the Malaysia–Singapore Second Link between AYE and Gelang Patah / Tanjung Kupang CIQ. Tuas is generally the fastest route if you are heading to Bukit Indah, Iskandar Puteri, Legoland Malaysia, Eco Botanic, or the North–South Expressway (PLUS) towards Malacca and Kuala Lumpur.

What each Tuas Checkpoint camera covers

  • Camera 4703 (Tuas Second Link Bridge): Shows the bridge deck connecting Tuas Checkpoint to Malaysia — ideal for spotting outbound car and motorcycle queues as well as inbound traffic entering Singapore.
  • Camera 4712 (Tuas Checkpoint approach): Covers the checkpoint complex approach along the Ayer Rajah Expressway (AYE).
  • Camera 4713 (AYE towards Tuas Checkpoint / JOHOR queue): Captures upstream tailbacks along the AYE before the immigration plaza so you can gauge heavy weekend or holiday build-ups early.

Keep in mind that Second Link carries higher toll charges than Woodlands Causeway, and indoor immigration counters can still queue even when bridge lanes look clear. Compare live conditions side-by-side on our Travel & Customs Hub or check Woodlands Checkpoint cameras before departing.

Tuas Checkpoint camera questions

Are the Tuas Checkpoint cameras live?
The images come from LTA and data.gov.sg feeds. Check the displayed update time because a feed can be delayed or unavailable.
Do the cameras show waiting time?
No. They show visible traffic conditions but do not calculate immigration or customs waiting times.
Is Tuas Checkpoint the Second Link?
Yes. Tuas Checkpoint serves the Malaysia-Singapore Second Link crossing towards Tanjung Kupang.
