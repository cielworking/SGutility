Second Link travel guide

Check Tuas Checkpoint traffic at the Second Link

Review the latest Tuas Checkpoint camera images before using the Malaysia-Singapore Second Link. The views can help you spot vehicle queues, rain and nearby road conditions before driving towards the border.

The images are visual snapshots rather than official waiting-time estimates. Check their timestamps and compare them with the Woodlands Checkpoint traffic cameras when either crossing is practical for your journey.

Tuas Checkpoint camera questions

Are the Tuas Checkpoint cameras live?
The images come from LTA and data.gov.sg feeds. Check the displayed update time because a feed can be delayed or unavailable.
Do the cameras show waiting time?
No. They show visible traffic conditions but do not calculate immigration or customs waiting times.
Is Tuas Checkpoint the Second Link?
Yes. Tuas Checkpoint serves the Malaysia-Singapore Second Link crossing towards Tanjung Kupang.
