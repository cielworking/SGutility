Travel planning guide

How to check Woodlands Checkpoint traffic before you leave

Use the latest Woodlands Checkpoint camera images to look for lane build-up, wet weather and congestion on the Singapore side of the Causeway. Each image includes a source update time, so check the timestamp before making a travel decision.

Traffic cameras are most useful as a visual check. They cannot show immigration processing time or conditions beyond the camera view. For a broader comparison, open the Tuas Checkpoint traffic cameras and consider your route to Johor Bahru.

Woodlands Checkpoint camera questions

Are the Woodlands Checkpoint cameras live?
The images come from LTA and data.gov.sg traffic-camera feeds. Check the displayed update time because images may be delayed or temporarily unavailable.
Do the cameras show the waiting time?
No. They show traffic build-up and road conditions but do not calculate an exact immigration waiting time.
Should I use Woodlands or Tuas?
Compare both camera pages, then consider your starting point, destination and current road conditions.
