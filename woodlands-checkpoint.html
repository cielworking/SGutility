Travel planning guide

How to read Woodlands Checkpoint cameras & beat Causeway jams

Use the live Woodlands Checkpoint camera feeds above to inspect lane build-up, motorcycle queues, heavy vehicles and wet weather along the Singapore approach to the Johor–Singapore Causeway. Our AI traffic panel counts visible cars, motorcycles and buses/trucks on Cam 2701 and Cam 2702 so you can spot sudden surges at a glance.

What each Woodlands Checkpoint camera shows

  • Camera 2701 (Woodlands Causeway towards Johor): Shows the main Causeway bridge deck and motorcycle lanes between Singapore and JB CIQ — the clearest indicator of outbound congestion into Johor Bahru.
  • Camera 2702 (Woodlands Checkpoint towards BKE): Covers the checkpoint exit and BKE approach viaduct, useful for checking queues entering Singapore or backing up onto the Bukit Timah Expressway.
  • Camera 2704 (BKE towards Woodlands Checkpoint): Shows upstream traffic along the BKE before the checkpoint slip roads so you can decide early whether to divert to Tuas Second Link.

Woodlands Checkpoint camera questions

Are the Woodlands Checkpoint cameras live?
The images come from LTA and data.gov.sg traffic-camera feeds. Check the displayed update time because images may be delayed or temporarily unavailable.
Do the cameras show the waiting time?
No. They show traffic build-up and road conditions but do not calculate an exact immigration waiting time.
Should I use Woodlands or Tuas?
Compare both camera pages, then consider your starting point, destination and current road conditions.
