Singapore TOTO questions

Draw days, prize groups and where the numbers come from.

When are Singapore TOTO draws held?
TOTO is drawn every Monday and Thursday. Each draw publishes six winning numbers and one additional number.
How many numbers do I need to match to win TOTO?
Prizes are based on how many of the six winning numbers you match, and the additional number is used for some of the lower prize groups. Match all six numbers to win the Group 1 prize.
Where do the TOTO results come from?
The page shows results published by Singapore Pools. Always check your ticket against the official Singapore Pools result before claiming a prize.
