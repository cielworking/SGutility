Fuel price guide

Compare Singapore petrol prices, grades (92, 95, 98, V-Power) & budget stations

Compare listed pump prices for Esso (Synergy Extra, Supreme+), Shell (FuelSave 95, 98 & Shell V-Power), SPC (LEVO 92, 95, 98), Caltex (Techron 92, 95 & Platinum 98), Sinopec (SINO X Power) and budget independent stations such as Smart Energy (and similar independent retailers like Cnergy) across RON 92, RON 95, RON 98, Premium and Diesel grades.

While independent stations and SPC frequently post the lowest headline board price before discounts, major retailers like Esso, Shell, Caltex and Sinopec often match or beat them once instant site discounts and monthly bank cashback (such as UOB One, OCBC 365, DBS Esso, HSBC or POSB Everyday) are stacked.

How to pick the cheapest fuel grade & station today

  • Check the green "Cheapest" badge in the live pump table above for each grade (92, 95, 98, Premium / Shell V-Power, Diesel).
  • Compare nett $/L with your actual card: Use our Petrol Discount Calculator or read our Best Credit Cards for Petrol in Singapore guide to check minimum spend tiers and cashback caps.
  • Driving to JB? Remember Singapore’s three-quarter tank petrol rule for SG-registered cars exiting via Woodlands or Tuas.

Singapore petrol price questions

Which petrol station is cheapest in Singapore today?
Smart Energy and SPC typically offer the lowest listed pump prices for 92, 95, 98 and Diesel in Singapore, while Sinopec often posts the lowest listed Premium grade price. Check the green Cheapest badges in the live table above for today's latest rates.
What is the Shell V-Power and Premium petrol price in Singapore?
Shell V-Power, Caltex Platinum 98 and Sinopec SINO X Power are listed under the Premium column in the live Singapore petrol price table above, updated hourly in SGD per litre before station and credit card discounts.
Which petrol brands are compared?
Available prices cover Esso, Shell (FuelSave & V-Power), SPC, Caltex, Sinopec and Smart Energy across 92, 95, 98, Premium and Diesel grades.
Are credit card discounts included in the listed pump price?
No. Pump prices and estimated effective prices are presented separately. Open the SG Alerts Petrol Discount Calculator to stack instant station discounts with credit card cashback.
How often can Singapore petrol prices change?
Retailers may update prices at any time. Check the update timestamp above and confirm the board price at the station before pumping.
