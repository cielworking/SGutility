Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information SG Alerts may process, why it is used and the choices available to visitors.

SG Alerts does not require an account and does not ask visitors to submit personal details to use the dashboard.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the SG Alerts website and describes the information processed when you browse the site or contact us.

2. Information we may collect

Information you provide

If you email us, we receive the email address, message and any information you choose to include. Please avoid sending sensitive personal information unless it is necessary.

Technical and usage information

Web hosting and analytics services may automatically process information such as browser type, device type, approximate location, referring page, pages viewed, date and time, and an internet protocol address or similar identifier.

SG Alerts does not knowingly use the dashboard to collect passwords, payment-card information or government identification numbers.

3. Google Analytics and cookies

SG Alerts uses Google Analytics to understand general website usage and improve the dashboard. Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to measure visits, navigation and device information.

Google handles analytics information under its own policies. You can review Google’s Privacy Policy or use the Google Analytics opt-out tool.

Your browser may also allow you to block or delete cookies. Some basic preferences—such as whether Singapore Pools results are hidden—may be stored locally in your browser.

4. Advertising (including Google AdSense)

SG Alerts may display advertisements served by third-party vendors, including Google, through Google AdSense or similar advertising partners.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user’s prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalised advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

These advertising partners may collect information such as cookie identifiers, approximate location, and pages visited in order to measure ad performance and, where permitted, personalise ads. SG Alerts does not control the cookies set by these third parties; please review their policies for details.

5. How information is used

  • Operate, secure and maintain the website.
  • Understand aggregated usage and improve page design and content.
  • Serve and measure advertisements (when ads are enabled).
  • Respond to feedback, correction requests or enquiries.
  • Investigate technical problems or misuse.

6. Service providers and disclosure

Information may be processed by service providers that support website hosting, analytics, advertising or security. We do not sell personal information.

Information may also be disclosed when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect rights and safety, or investigate abuse of the service.

7. Retention and security

Contact messages and technical records are retained only for as long as reasonably needed for their purpose, operational requirements or legal obligations. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices

You may restrict analytics cookies through browser controls or the Google opt-out tool. You may manage advertising personalisation via Google Ads Settings and www.aboutads.info. You may also contact us to ask about personal information contained in a message you previously sent, subject to applicable legal requirements.

9. External links and children

External websites have their own privacy practices, which SG Alerts does not control. SG Alerts is a general-audience information service and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

10. Updates and contact

We may revise this policy as the website changes. Questions or privacy requests may be sent to support@sgalerts.com.
