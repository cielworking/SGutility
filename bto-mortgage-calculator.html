BTO planning guide

How to plan your HDB BTO budget before applying

Before submitting a BTO application, obtain your HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter early to confirm your exact Enhanced CPF Housing Grant (EHG) amount and maximum HDB or bank housing loan.

Check both your 30% Mortgage Servicing Ratio (MSR) and monthly CPF Ordinary Account contributions so your cash top-up stays comfortable even if interest rates change.

BTO mortgage questions

What is the Mortgage Servicing Ratio (MSR) for HDB BTO flats?
Monthly HDB mortgage payments are capped at 30% of gross monthly household income under MAS MSR rules.
What is the current HDB concessionary housing loan interest rate?
2.6% per annum (0.1% above the prevailing CPF Ordinary Account interest rate).
Do I need an HFE letter before applying for a BTO launch?
Yes. Applicants must hold a valid HFE letter before submitting a BTO application.
