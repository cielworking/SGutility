Singapore Speed Limits & Enforcement

Types of Speed Cameras & Speeding Penalties in Singapore

The Singapore Police Force (SPF) deploys fixed speed cameras (painted in high-visibility orange and white stripes), mobile speed cameras along accident-prone stretches, Police Speed Laser Cameras (PSLC), and digital red-light cameras with speed enforcement activated across major junctions.

Singapore speed camera questions

How many official SPF speed enforcement cameras are in Singapore?
The Singapore Police Force (SPF) publishes 100 official speed enforcement camera coordinates across expressways (PIE, AYE, CTE, ECP, BKE, SLE, KJE, TPE) and major arterial roads.
What are the speeding fines and demerit points in Singapore?
Under Singapore Traffic Police rules, speeding by 1–20 km/h carries 4 demerit points and a $150–$200 fine for light vehicles; 21–30 km/h carries 6 points; 31–40 km/h carries 8 points; and exceeding by more than 40 km/h leads to court prosecution and 12–24 demerit points.
Do Singapore red-light cameras also detect speeding?
Yes. The Singapore Police Force progressively activates the speed enforcement function in digital red-light cameras across junctions islandwide to deter speeding through intersections.
