Singapore 4D questions

Draw days, prize tiers and where the numbers come from.

When are Singapore 4D draws held?
4D is drawn every Wednesday, Saturday and Sunday. Each draw publishes the top three prize numbers together with the starter and consolation numbers.
What do the 4D prize tiers mean?
The four-digit number matching the first prize wins the top prize tier, followed by the second and third prize numbers. Starter and consolation numbers carry smaller prizes, and the actual payout depends on your bet type and stake.
Where do the 4D results come from?
The page shows results published by Singapore Pools. Always check your ticket against the official Singapore Pools result before claiming a prize.
