Key Differences

HDB BTO vs HDB Resale Comparison

Factor HDB BTO (New Launch) HDB Resale (Open Market)
Pricing & Grants Subsidised launch prices; Enhanced CPF Housing Grant (EHG) up to $120,000 for eligible families. Market-negotiated price; first-timers can stack Family Grant + EHG + Proximity Housing Grant (PHG).
Waiting Time Typically 3–5 years construction (Shorter Waiting Time flats ~2–3 years). Immediate key collection after ~3–4 months resale completion.
Upfront Cash Risk No Cash Over Valuation (COV); downpayment largely coverable via CPF OA + grants. Any price agreed above HDB valuation (COV) must be paid 100% in cash.
Minimum Occupation Period (MOP) 5 years for Standard flats; 10 years for Plus and Prime BTO projects (plus subsidy clawback). 5 years for Standard resale flats (or 10 years if buying a Prime/Plus resale flat).
Income Ceiling $14,000/month for families ($7,000 singles) on BTO applications. No household income ceiling to purchase resale (ceilings apply to grants / HDB loans).

Common Housing Questions & Guides ("What Is…")

What is an HFE Letter and when do I need one?

The HDB Flat Eligibility (HFE) letter assesses in one step whether you qualify to buy a new or resale flat, how much CPF housing grant you receive, and your HDB loan quantum. Apply early because processing takes weeks—you cannot submit a BTO ballot or request an HDB resale valuation without a valid HFE letter.

What is Cash Over Valuation (COV) when buying HDB Resale?

After you exercise/accept an agreed resale price with a seller, HDB conducts an official valuation. If you offer $620,000 and HDB values the flat at $600,000, the $20,000 gap is COV. HDB loans and CPF OA funds cannot finance COV, so buyers must hold extra cash reserves before negotiating.

What is MSR 30% vs TDSR 55%?

For HDB flats (BTO or Resale), the Mortgage Servicing Ratio (MSR) limits monthly housing loan instalments to 30% of gross monthly household income. On top of that, Total Debt Servicing Ratio (TDSR) caps all monthly debt obligations (car loans, credit lines, student loans, mortgages) at 55% of gross income.

What do Standard, Plus, and Prime HDB BTO categories mean?

BTO launches are grouped into Standard (5-year MOP, no resale income ceiling), Plus (choicer locations near MRT/town centres, 10-year MOP, subsidy recovery on first resale), and Prime (central/city-fringe, tightest 10-year MOP and highest subsidy recovery).
