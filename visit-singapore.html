Top Places to Go

Essential Singapore Attractions

Short pocket breakdown with nearest MRT stations, admission status, and local timing tips:

Gardens by the Bay

Free Grounds

Futuristic Supertree Grove with free nightly light shows (7:45pm & 8:45pm), plus ticketed Flower Dome and Cloud Forest indoor waterfall.

Nearest MRT: Gardens by the Bay (TE22) / Bayfront (DT16/CE1) Best Time: 5:30 PM (explore domes, stay for light show)

Marina Bay & Merlion Park

Free Entry

Iconic Singapore skyline loop. Stroll from the Merlion statue along the Helix Bridge to Marina Bay Sands promenade for Spectra light & water show.

Nearest MRT: Raffles Place (EW14/NS26) / Bayfront (DT16) Best Time: 7:00 PM (sunset breeze & glittering towers)

Jewel Changi Airport

Free Entry

World's tallest indoor waterfall (HSBC Rain Vortex) surrounded by lush 4-storey indoor forest and Canopy Park. Visit right upon landing or before departure.

Nearest MRT: Changi Airport (CG2) linked to T1, T2, T3 Best Time: Day or night (light & sound show hourly)

Chinatown & Kampong Glam

Free Walkable

Rich cultural heritage quarters. Chinatown's Buddha Tooth Relic Temple & Maxwell Hawker Centre, paired with Arab Street's Sultan Mosque and Haji Lane street art.

Nearest MRT: Chinatown (DT19/NE4) & Bugis (EW12/DT14) Best Time: 11:00 AM – 3:00 PM for food; 6:00 PM for nightlife

Mandai Wildlife Reserves

Ticketed

World-renowned Singapore Zoo, Night Safari (tram through nocturnal animal habitats), River Wonders (giant pandas), and Bird Paradise.

Nearest MRT: Khatib (NS14) + Mandai Khatib Shuttle (S$1) Best Time: Morning for Zoo/Birds; 7:15 PM for Night Safari

Sentosa Island & Beaches

Free Beaches

Island resort with free Siloso & Palawan sandy beaches, plus ticketed Universal Studios Singapore (USS), S.E.A. Aquarium, and cable car rides.

Nearest MRT: HarbourFront (NE1/CC29) + Sentosa Express Best Time: Full day / Beach sunset

Good to Know

10-Second Singapore Tourist Essentials

No Tipping Required

Restaurants already add a 10% service charge + 9% GST ("++"). Hawker centres and taxis do not expect tips.

Tap Water Is Drinkable

Singapore tap water meets WHO standards and is safe to drink directly from the tap — bring a refillable bottle.

Power Plugs: Type G (230V)

UK-style 3-rectangular-pin plugs. Most modern hotels also include USB-A/USB-C ports bedside.

SIM / eSIM & Free Wi-Fi

Pick up a 100GB tourist SIM (~S$12) at Changi or use an eSIM. Free #WiFi@Changi & Wireless@SGx cover airports and malls.

Smart Planning

Classic 3-Day Singapore Pocket Itinerary

Day 1: Civic Centre & Marina Bay

  • Morning: Fort Canning Tree Tunnel & National Gallery Singapore.
  • Lunch: Lau Pa Sat hawker market (chicken rice & satay).
  • Afternoon: Marina Bay Sands Shoppes & ArtScience Museum.
  • Evening: Supertree Grove light show at Gardens by the Bay.

Day 2: Heritage & Retail Strip

  • Morning: Chinatown Buddha Tooth Relic Temple & Maxwell food.
  • Afternoon: Kampong Glam Sultan Mosque & Haji Lane boutiques.
  • Late Afternoon: Orchard Road malls for shopping (claim GST refunds).
  • Evening: Clarke Quay riverside dinner & Singapore River cruise.

Day 3: Nature, Wildlife & Sentosa

  • Morning: Singapore Botanic Gardens (UNESCO World Heritage Site).
  • Afternoon: Sentosa Island beach relaxing or Universal Studios.
  • Evening: Mandai Night Safari or Jewel Changi Rain Vortex before flight.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many days do you need in Singapore for a first visit?
3 to 4 days allows you to comfortably explore the top attractions, hawker centres, museums, and natural gardens without rushing.
What is the best way to travel around Singapore as a tourist?
The MRT subway is fastest and cheapest. Tap your foreign credit card or smartphone mobile wallet directly via SimplyGo.
What is the best neighborhood to stay in Singapore?
Bugis and Chinatown offer the best mix of central transit, food, and reasonable rates. Marina Bay is top for luxury.
