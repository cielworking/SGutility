Fixed Deposit, T-Bill & SSB questions
What is the latest 6-month Singapore T-Bill cut-off yield?
The latest MAS 6-month Singapore T-Bill cut-off yield is pulled directly from Monetary Authority of Singapore auction records in the live table above.
Are Singapore Savings Bonds (SSB) and T-Bills safer than bank fixed deposits?
Both SSB and T-Bills are 100% backed by the Singapore Government, whereas SGD fixed deposits at SDIC-member banks are insured up to S$100,000 per depositor per bank.
Can I withdraw SSB or T-Bills early if I need cash?
SSBs can be redeemed any month with accrued pro-rated interest and zero penalty. T-Bills must either be held for the full 6 months or sold on the secondary market at a local bank branch (price may vary).
