Housing launch guide

Plan for upcoming BTO launches in Singapore

Use the town and flat-type overview to identify upcoming BTO launches that may fit your household needs. The page summarises the latest available HDB Flat Portal information and groups multiple projects within the same town.

Early launch information can change. Project names, exact supply, classification, waiting time, eligibility details and prices may only be confirmed when HDB publishes the sales launch. Check the official HDB announcement before applying.
