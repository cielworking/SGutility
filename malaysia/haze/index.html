Understanding malaysia haze & air quality

Malaysia reports the Air Pollutant Index (API), also called Indeks Pencemaran Udara (IPU), by monitoring station. Select a state to find nearby stations. The displayed category comes from DOE. API / IPU and Singapore PSI are different national indices; compare their labels and reporting periods rather than treating the values as interchangeable. These are reported readings, not a haze forecast.

How current is this information?

Always check the forecast validity or reporting time. Older cached readings remain visible with a warning when a source is delayed or unavailable. Times use Singapore / Malaysia time (UTC+08).

Where can I check official updates?

Visit Department of Environment Malaysia / APIMS for the latest official information and advisories.
