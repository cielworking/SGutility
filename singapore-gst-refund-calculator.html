How to Claim Your GST Refund in 4 Simple Steps

1

Shop with Passport

Spend at least S$100 at an eTRS merchant. Show physical passport (not copy) at checkout so transactions are tied to your passport number.

2

Before Check-in

If goods are going into check-in luggage, validate at the Changi eTRS self-help kiosk in the Departure Check-in Hall before dropping bags.

3

Scan at eTRS Kiosk

Scan your passport at the automated kiosk. Select all transactions. Keep purchased items readily accessible in case Customs requests inspection.

4

Collect Refund

Choose direct refund to credit card (within 10 days) or collect instant cash at the Central Refund Counter inside the transit lounge.

Official IRAS Rules

Tourist GST Refund Eligibility & Exceptions

Under the Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS) Electronic Tourist Refund Scheme (eTRS), foreign tourists are entitled to a refund on the 9% Goods and Services Tax (GST) paid on purchases brought out of Singapore.

Who Qualifies?

  • You are 16 years of age or older at the time of purchase.
  • You are not a Singapore Citizen or Permanent Resident.
  • You do not hold an Employment Pass, S Pass, Work Permit, Dependant's Pass, or Student Pass.
  • You are departing Singapore via Changi Airport or Seletar Airport within 2 months from date of purchase.

What Cannot Be Claimed?

  • Services (hotel stays, car rentals, dining/hawker food, spa treatments).
  • Goods already consumed or partly consumed in Singapore.
  • Goods intended for commercial purposes or export via freight.
  • Departures via land (Woodlands or Tuas checkpoint to Malaysia are strictly not eligible for eTRS).

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum spend to qualify for Singapore tourist GST refund?
You must spend at least S$100 (incl. GST) at any single participating retailer. You can accumulate up to 3 same-day receipts from the same shop.
Do I get the entire 9% GST back as a tourist?
No. The central refund handling agencies (Global Blue / Planet) charge a processing administration fee. The effective refund is typically ~5.5% to 7.0% of the gross receipt amount.
Can I claim GST refund if I leave Singapore by land (Causeway / Tuas) or cruise?
No for land borders. eTRS is strictly valid for air departures (Changi/Seletar) and international cruises from designated cruise terminals.
