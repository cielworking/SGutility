About the dashboard

Useful Singapore information, in one place

SG Alerts is an independent dashboard designed to make everyday public information faster to find and easier to understand.

Open dashboard

Easy to scan

Important traffic, motoring, housing and public information is organised into one clear dashboard.

Source-led

Where available, information is drawn from official data feeds and linked back to its source for verification.

Independent

SG Alerts is independently operated and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any Singapore government agency.

What you can find here

The dashboard currently brings together Singapore news alerts, live Woodlands and Tuas checkpoint cameras, petrol prices and discount estimates, COE bidding results, upcoming BTO information, Singapore Pools results and practical public reminders.

How information is presented

SG Alerts aims to show useful summaries without hiding the original source. Time-sensitive information includes source links or update timestamps so visitors can verify details before making a decision.

Corrections and feedback

If something appears inaccurate, outdated or unclear, please send the page and relevant details to support@sgalerts.com. Corrections help keep the dashboard useful for everyone.
