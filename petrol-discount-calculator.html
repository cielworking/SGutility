Fuel savings guide

How to get the lowest net petrol price in Singapore

Headline pump prices at major retailers often sit within a few cents of each other, so card discounts and loyalty stacking drive most of your actual fuel savings. Compare station discounts carefully against monthly minimum spend rules.

Fixed-rate stations such as Smart Energy publish direct nett pump prices without requiring minimum credit card spend, which can beat bank headline percentages for lower-mileage drivers.

Petrol calculator questions

How are net Singapore petrol prices calculated in this calculator?
The calculator reads today's published pump prices and applies each station's listed bank credit card or loyalty discount percentage (or direct nett price) to estimate net $/L, per-tank cost, and annual savings.
Which petrol grade should most Singapore cars use: RON 92, 95 or 98?
Most cars in Singapore run on RON 95, while some standard engines accept RON 92 and high-compression engines may require RON 98. Check your fuel cap or owner's manual.
Why can effective savings vary from the headline bank discount percentage?
Bank promotions often combine upfront station rebates with statement cashback subject to monthly minimum spend and cashback caps.
