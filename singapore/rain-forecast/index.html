Understanding singapore rain forecast

The two-hour forecast describes expected weather by area. It is a forecast, not a measurement of rain already fallen or an exact rain-start time. Check the validity window before planning your journey.

How current is this information?

Always check the forecast validity or reporting time. Older cached readings remain visible with a warning when a source is delayed or unavailable. Times use Singapore / Malaysia time (UTC+08).

Where can I check official updates?

Visit Meteorological Service Singapore / data.gov.sg for the latest official information and advisories.
