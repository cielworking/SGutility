What do these conditions mean?

Cloudy: cloud-covered skies are forecast; the word alone does not specify rain. Showers: rain is expected within the stated area and period. Thundery showers: showers with thunder are forecast. These are period forecasts, not exact rain-start times at your address.

Understanding singapore rain forecast

Explore Singapore and Malaysia together using observed rain radar, Singapore's official five-region forecast periods, and Malaysia location forecasts. Choose an individual Singapore area for the two-hour forecast. Radar playback shows recent observations; it does not predict exact rain arrival times.

How current is this information?

Always check the forecast validity or reporting time. Older cached readings remain visible with a warning when a source is delayed or unavailable. Times use Singapore / Malaysia time (UTC+08).

Where can I check official updates?

Visit Meteorological Service Singapore / data.gov.sg for the latest official information and advisories.
